CrackNEET Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

CrackNEET is an exam-preparation application. If you create an account, we collect your name and email and store your password as a one-way hash. We also store test attempts, scores and progress to provide account-based features.

Use of information

Information is used to authenticate users, save progress, provide tests and improve reliability. We do not sell personal information.

Security

Production API traffic is served over HTTPS. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes.

Account deletion

Users can delete their account from the account/profile area. Account data and associated test attempts are permanently deleted.

Contact

Privacy questions can be submitted through the developer contact shown on Google Play.
