CrackNEET Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

CrackNEET is an exam-practice application for NEET and JEE Main preparation.

Information we collect

The current version is designed to work without an account and does not request access to contacts, SMS, call logs, location, camera, microphone, or device files. Practice data may be stored locally on the device for app functionality.

How information is used

Locally stored practice information is used to provide question practice, scores, bookmarks and progress features when those features are enabled.

Sharing

The current app does not intentionally sell or share user personal information with third parties.

Children

CrackNEET is an educational exam-preparation app. Parents/guardians should review the app experience for younger users.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app functionality changes. The effective date will be updated above.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the contact details shown on the Google Play listing.
